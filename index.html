Every restaurant on Devon Avenue, in one honest index.

Devon Avenue on Chicago's far north side holds one of the densest runs of Indian, Pakistani, and Bangladeshi restaurants in the country — biryani houses next to chaat counters next to sweet shops. There's no good map of it. We're walking the avenue block by block and writing one: a plain-text index, organized by cuisine, with the details that actually matter when you're choosing where to eat.

House rules No paid placement. No star ratings. No listicles. A restaurant gets in by being on Devon and being open — and gets described, not scored.

How we list

Three rules make the index worth trusting.

We eat first, write second

No entry is compiled from menus or other websites. If we haven't sat down and ordered, the restaurant isn't in the edition yet.

Described, never scored

No stars, no rankings, no "best of." Each entry says what a kitchen does well and who it suits, and lets you choose.

Free to be listed, always

Restaurants can't pay to appear, to rank, or to edit their entry. Corrections of fact are always welcome; rewrites are not for sale.
